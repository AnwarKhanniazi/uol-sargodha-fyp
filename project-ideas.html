Project Ideas

Faculty-suggested domains for this batch. Groups may also propose an original topic — see the note at the bottom.

Web & Mobile

Smart Campus Services

Systems for hostel/transport booking, lost & found, or attendance automation tailored to campus operations.

AI / ML

Applied Machine Learning

Crop yield or disease prediction for Sargodha's citrus and wheat belt, student performance prediction, or local-language NLP.

Health Tech

Healthcare Support Tools

Appointment and referral systems for local clinics, medication reminders, or basic diagnostic-support tools built with supervisor sign-off on data ethics.

IoT

Embedded & IoT Systems

Irrigation monitoring for orchard farms, smart energy metering, or low-cost environmental sensing relevant to the region's agriculture.

Security

Cybersecurity & Networks

Vulnerability scanning tools, secure authentication schemes, or network monitoring dashboards for small/medium organizations.

E-commerce

Local Marketplace Platforms

Marketplace or logistics platforms connecting Sargodha's fruit/produce traders with buyers, including order tracking and vendor verification.

Games & Simulation

Educational Games

Interactive tools that teach programming, math, or science concepts to school-age students in the region.

Data Systems

Data Engineering & Dashboards

ETL pipelines and dashboards for university admissions data, alumni tracking, or research-lab equipment scheduling.

Have your own idea?

Groups are encouraged to propose an original topic outside this list. Submit a one-page summary (problem, proposed approach, expected outcome) with your Group Registration Form on the Forms page — the FYP committee reviews custom proposals within one week.
