Writing your report

Documentation Template

The official structure and formatting rules for your final FYP report. Use this template from your first draft, not just the final one.

Download template (DOCX)
Required structure

Chapters your report must include

#ChapterWhat it covers
1IntroductionProblem statement, motivation, objectives, scope
2Literature ReviewRelated work and how your project differs
3Requirements & AnalysisFunctional/non-functional requirements, use cases
4System DesignArchitecture, ER/class diagrams, technology stack
5ImplementationKey modules, algorithms, notable engineering decisions
6Testing & EvaluationTest cases, results, performance where relevant
7Conclusion & Future WorkOutcomes against objectives, limitations, next steps
—References & AppendicesCitations, user manual, extra diagrams/code listings
Formatting

Page setup

A4 size, 1-inch margins, 1.5 line spacing, 12pt body text in Times New Roman, page numbers bottom-center.

Length

Target length

50–90 pages excluding appendices, scaled to project complexity — quality over padding.

Citations

Reference style

IEEE citation style throughout, with a consistently numbered reference list.

Originality

Similarity check

Must be under the 19% similarity threshold defined in the Guidelines page before submission is accepted.
