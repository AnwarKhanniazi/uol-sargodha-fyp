FYP Guidelines

Eligibility, supervision, and evaluation rules that apply to every group in the Department of Computer Science & Software Engineering.

Eligibility

Who can start FYP

Students must have cleared all coursework up to the 6th semester with no outstanding F grades, and must be enrolled in the final two semesters of their degree.

Groups

Group size & formation

Groups consist of 2–3 students from the same degree program. Solo projects require written approval from the FYP coordinator and a stronger technical justification.

Supervision

Choosing a supervisor

Each group is paired with one internal supervisor based on domain fit. Groups may suggest a preferred supervisor, but final allocation rests with the FYP committee to balance faculty load.

Meetings

Progress check-ins

Groups must meet their supervisor at least once every two weeks and log the discussion in the Progress Report form, available on the Forms page.

Originality

Plagiarism & originality

Final documentation is checked against a similarity threshold of 19%. Submissions above this threshold are returned for revision before grading can proceed.

Scope changes

Changing your topic

Topic or scope changes after the Proposal Defense require a written request to the FYP committee and are only approved before the Mid-Term Evaluation.

Evaluation

How each stage is graded

StageWeightAssessed by
Proposal Defense10%FYP Committee
Mid-Term Evaluation20%Internal Panel
Final Defense40%Internal + External Examiner
Documentation Quality15%Supervisor
Supervisor Assessment (process & conduct)15%Supervisor

Exact rubrics for each stage are shared by supervisors ahead of the relevant evaluation and posted on the Notifications page.

Professional & ethical conduct

Every group is expected to handle data responsibly, credit third-party code and datasets, and disclose any use of AI tools in development or writing where the supervisor requires it. Misrepresenting authorship or project scope is treated as a disciplinary matter, not a grading deduction.
