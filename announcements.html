FYP Announcements

Time-sensitive updates from the FYP committee — deadlines, venue changes, and evaluation schedules.

July 28, 2026 · Update

Forms Page Cleaned Up; Official Project Domains Published

Forms 1–12 (supervisor approval/withdrawal, change of title/supervisor, extension, meeting follow-up, attendance, external advisor, re-evaluation, and observer evaluation) have been taken down from the Forms page pending reissue in an updated format — contact the FYP office if you need one before it's reposted. The Project Ideas page now lists the department's official approved domains with research-based and project-based example titles for each, replacing the earlier illustrative list.

July 28, 2026

Presentation Formats Now Split by Track; LaTeX Template Pack Added

FYP-I and FYP-II presentation decks are now available separately for the Application and Research tracks on the Forms page. A four-template LaTeX pack (covering both phases and tracks, with an accompanying Student's Guide) has also been added to the Documentation page alongside the existing DOCX template.

July 27, 2026

Guidance for Post-Evaluation Revisions & Project Titles Published

The Guidelines page now covers the revision process after a Proposal Defense, Mid-Evaluation, or Final Defense/Viva, including the Revision Tracking Sheet and resubmission checklist. Title formation rules for both project tracks are also now posted there.

July 22, 2026 · Heads up Action needed

Fall 2026 FYP-1 & FYP-2 Timeline Published

The full FYP-1 and FYP-2 schedule for the Fall 2026 semester is now live on the Timeline page. Group formation and the problem-domain guideline both open in Week 2 of the semester (Sep 28) — start thinking about your topic now.

July 15, 2026

Proposal & Final Defense Presentation Formats Released

Official slide formats for both the Proposal Defense and Final Defense are now available on the Forms page. All groups must present using these formats — panels will flag non-compliant decks.

July 8, 2026

Updated Documentation Template Released

A revised documentation template is now available on the Documentation page, with clearer formatting for the literature review and testing chapters. AI-generated reports are not accepted for FYP-1 or FYP-2 submissions.

June 25, 2026

Problem Domain Guidance Coming in Week 2

The FYP Committee Head will issue formal guidance on selecting a problem domain, alongside the call for FYP titles, in Week 2 of the Fall semester (Sep 28). Groups are encouraged to start informal brainstorming ahead of that date.

June 10, 2026

Fall 2026 FYP Cycle Confirmed

The Examination Department has confirmed the FYP-1/FYP-2 cycle for the Fall 2026 semester. Group formation and the Submission of Approval Form both open in Week 2 — watch this page for the exact opening date.

Don't miss an update

Announcements posted here are also shared with supervisors to relay in class. If a deadline here conflicts with what your supervisor told you, this page is the authoritative source.
